If you are 14 to 17 and live in Spain, you can register and give consent yourself. Accounts for children aged 7 to 13, which a parent or legal guardian would have to create, are not available yet. Nobody under 7 can have an account.

What can other people see?

Your Profile starts private: other people see only your username. You choose whether it stays private. If you make it unlisted, anyone who has its link can see it, even without signing in. If you make it public, people signed in to __APP_NAME__ can also find it by your name or username, your sports, whether you are an athlete, coach or referee, your professions, whether you are available now and the place you show, and search engines can show it. An unlisted or public Profile shows everything you have added: your name, pictures, biography, gender and weight, your sports, how many fights you say you have had as an athlete in each sport, the Organizations, coaches and athletes you are connected to, your sport experience and your professional roles. You also appear on the page of an Organization you are affiliated with, and on the Profile of your coach or your athletes, when theirs is visible too. So only add what you are happy for others to see. Turning 18 does not change your choice.

In that search you appear exactly as an adult does, and only while your Profile is public. People who find you see your username, name, picture, sports and roles, how many fights you say you have had as an athlete in each sport, whether you are available now and the place you show. Nobody you have blocked, or who has blocked you, finds you. We do not suggest people to anyone or show people on a map.

You can also add a place to your Profile in Settings: a town or city you pick from a list, never where your phone is. Nobody sees it until you choose to show it. When you show it, people see it on your Profile and in search, with how many kilometres away it is from the place they searched from. When it is hidden, only you use it, to search near it. To find places, our server asks Google Maps: Google Maps gets what you type in the list, never who you are or your phone's internet address.

In the journal you choose, for each entry, who sees it. A public entry — a photo, a video, a text, a poll or a moment — can be seen by everyone signed in to __APP_NAME__, with your username, and with your name and picture while your Profile is public or unlisted. So can your comments, and your vote in a poll. A private entry or a draft is only for you. Nothing in the journal can be seen by people who are not signed in, or found in search engines.

You can also comment on the page of a verified event. Everyone signed in to __APP_NAME__ can read that comment, with your username, and with your name and picture while your Profile is public or unlisted, and the owners and admins of the Organization that organizes the event are told about it and can delete it. It can never be seen by people who are not signed in, or found in search engines, even though the event's page can.

A public moment shows on a map the exact place where you created it, so think before sharing where you are. You can delete your entries and comments at any time, report anything that worries you, and block someone so that neither of you sees the other. Our team reviews reports and removes what breaks the rules. A parent or guardian cannot publish or act for you in the journal.

Who can write to you?

You can send text messages to other people, one to one or in groups, and they can write to you. If your Profile is public, people signed in can find you by your name to write to you; otherwise they can find you only if they type your full username. They see your name only while your Profile is public or unlisted. Anyone who finds you can write to you without asking first, and a group's admin can add you to a group, so share your username only with people you know. You can leave any group whenever you want.

Messages are kept on our computers and are not locked so that only the people in the conversation can read them (they are not end-to-end encrypted). The people in the conversation read them, and our team reads a message only if someone reports it. You can delete your own messages for everyone, report a message that worries you, and block someone so that they cannot write to you one to one. If a message worries you, talk to an adult you trust.

We keep your account, profile, active declarations, and active consent information while the account exists and it is needed to provide the Service. A registration grant expires after 15 minutes. Temporary records are removed when used, and expired or cancelled temporary records are deleted within 24 hours.

We delete the code Apple gave us within 25 hours once it is no longer linked to your account. When your account is deleted, we first use it to ask Apple to end our access.

What you publish stays until you delete it or delete your account. When an entry is deleted or removed, its content goes at once, and a small record of it is deleted 30 days later. Notifications are deleted after 90 days at most, or 30 days after you read them. An email we are going to send you keeps your address only until it is sent, and never more than 24 hours, even when it tells you that your account was deleted. Your messages stay while the conversation exists, until you delete them; a deleted message goes at once for everyone, and a small record of it is deleted 30 days later. Deleting your account deletes every message you sent.

A request to verify an event, and our reviewers' notes on it, are deleted with the event, and stay with it if its Organization is deleted.

A chief-referee offer or role that was rejected, withdrawn or ended is kept privately for up to 24 months, then deleted. Deleting your account deletes the ones offered to you earlier.

A referee convocation is kept with its event and deleted with it. Deleting your account deletes your convocations as a referee earlier.

A comment on an event is kept until you, the people who manage the event or our team delete it, and is deleted with the event or with your account.

An invitation to take part in an event is kept with the event and deleted with it. A copy of your data you asked for before then keeps it until that copy is deleted, 7 days after it is ready. Deleting your account deletes your invitations as an athlete earlier.

You decide whether to do it. If you are changing your date of birth and you do not do it, your date of birth stays as it is. We do not keep the photos of your document, your selfie or the information about your face, and we tell the company to delete everything it collected for the check soon after it gives us the result.
