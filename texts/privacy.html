4. Data Retention

We retain account, profile, and active consent data while the account exists and as needed to provide the Service. Registration grants expire after 15 minutes and minor invitations after 24 hours; their readable values are not stored. Temporary registration and provider-verification details are removed when consumed, and expired or revoked temporary records and invitation hashes are purged within 24 hours. When an account is deleted, the account and associated personal content are removed from the Service, and we do not recover a deleted account, even if you ask us to. Minimum billing, dispute, safety, or compliance records are retained only where a documented legal basis and deletion deadline apply.

A Sign in with Apple refresh token is kept, encrypted, while its Apple account is linked to a __APP_NAME__ account, and is deleted within 25 hours once no account is linked to it. When an account is deleted, we ask Apple to revoke __APP_NAME__'s access with each of its kept tokens. Until Apple answers, and never longer than 24 hours, we keep the encrypted token with the app or website it was issued to, and nothing that refers to the deleted account.

We back up the database every day, and before some maintenance work, so that we can recover the Service after a failure or an error. We rely on our proportionate legitimate interests in service security. Each backup holds the personal data in the database at that moment, including data deleted afterwards, and cannot be changed to remove a single item. We keep backups in Google Cloud and use them only to recover the Service and to test its recovery or maintenance. We delete them after 14 or 15 days, and Google Cloud keeps them recoverable for 3 more days, so what is deleted from the Service, an account included, normally leaves the backups within 18 days. What this Policy says about periods and deletion refers to data in the Service, not to these backups. If we ever had to restore a backup, we would delete again, before reopening the Service, the accounts deleted after it was made; other content deleted since then, such as an entry or a message, could reappear.

Profile images remain in the private managed-media library while the account exists or until you delete them. An event's cover image remains in that library while its event holds it, until a manager of the event's Organization replaces or removes it or deletes the event; it stays with its event when that Organization or an account is deleted. Logical deletion removes product access immediately and physical object deletion is retried until complete. A signed image link already issued can remain usable only until its original expiry, which is no longer than 15 minutes.

Journal entries, comments, likes, poll votes, lists, and blocks remain until you delete them or the account is deleted. When an entry ends — because you delete it, moderation removes it, or its video cannot be prepared — its text, caption, photo, video, poll, and location are cleared at once and its media is deleted at Cloudflare; a record holding only the author, the entry type, and timestamps is deleted 30 days later. A report that is decided without removing anything, and the minimum record of a moderation removal (the content reference, reason, decision, reviewer, and timestamps), are deleted no later than 30 days after the decision.

A message remains while its conversation exists, until its author deletes it or moderation removes it. Its text is then cleared at once for everyone, and a record holding only its author and timestamps is deleted 30 days later. A report about a message that is decided without removing it is deleted no later than 30 days after the decision. When an account is deleted, every message it sent and its place in every conversation are deleted; a one-to-one conversation with nobody left in it is deleted, and a group goes on for its other members.

An unread notification is deleted 90 days after it is created, and a read one 30 days after it is read or 90 days after it is created, whichever comes first. A push installation is removed when you sign out on that device, when Apple or Google reports its token invalid, or when the account is deleted. The text of a push exists only in the delivered message; once delivered, it cannot be withdrawn from the device.

An email waiting to be sent keeps your address and the email's language and content only until it is sent or cannot be delivered, and never longer than 24 hours after it is created. When an account is deleted, an email to it that is still waiting, such as the one confirming the deletion, keeps the address no longer than those 24 hours. What Cloudflare keeps after sending is described in section 3.

After an identity check we keep its result, its date, the reason we asked for it, the provider's reference for it, and the record of the consent you gave for it, while the account exists, and delete them with the account. The date of birth on the document of a check that passes becomes your account's date of birth. We never keep photos of your document, your selfie, or any biometric data. We use the name read from your document only to settle the case it was checked for, without keeping it afterwards. We instruct the provider to delete everything it collected for a check (the photos, the selfie and the frames of the liveness check, the biometric data, the details it read from your document, and the device and connection data) once it has returned the result to us, within the shortest period our agreement with it allows, and to delete in the same way what it collected for a check that was started and not finished. We will state that period when we name the provider.

Profile-name and custom-Bio validation metadata and Profile-status audit records remain private while the account exists and are deleted with it. OpenAI application-state storage is disabled for validation requests. Unless separately approved provider controls apply, OpenAI may retain limited abuse-monitoring data for up to 30 days; we do not use API content to train models.

A private account-lifecycle audit may survive deletion in detached, minimised form for up to 30 days for account-lifecycle integrity, based on our proportionate legitimate interests and subject to final legal review. On deletion, exact timestamps and detailed device and app versions are removed; the record is reduced to UTC calendar dates, client platform, operating-system family, and deletion reason.
